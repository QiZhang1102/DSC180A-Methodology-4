## Quarter 2 Project Brainstorming

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic for me was E-STEER, which explores how emotions can be represented and controlled inside large language models. I was especially interested in using sparse autoencoders to identify features related to valence, arousal, and dominance, and how changing these features might influence model behavior.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would like to investigate how steering different emotional dimensions affects the reasoning performance of LLMs. Building on our E-STEER reproduction using Qwen3-1.7B, I would test different steering strengths across all three VAD dimensions and examine how they affect both the model's emotional responses and its reasoning accuracy.

**3. What is a potential change you’d make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

Our Quarter 1 project focused on steering one emotional dimension using a relatively small model and a logical reasoning benchmark. I would expand the experiments to cover all three VAD dimensions, use more test questions, and compare results across multiple runs to improve reliability.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in comparing SAE-based steering with contrastive activation addition and prompt-based emotion control. I would also like to explore layer-by-layer activation analysis to better understand where emotional information is represented and how it influences model outputs.
